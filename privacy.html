LAUNCH CAROUSEL BUILDER

Privacy.

SHORT VERSION  This tool collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no cookies and no server to send anything to. Your words and images never leave your browser.

What happens to what you type

It stays on your device. Every part of this tool runs in your browser — the editing, the layout, the image processing and the export. There is no backend, so there is nowhere for your text to be sent even if we wanted it.

What happens to images you add

They are never uploaded. An image you add is read by your browser, resized and re-encoded locally, and kept inside your own saved deck. It is not transmitted anywhere. If you clear your browser data, it is gone — we have no copy, because we never had one.

Saved work

Your deck is saved in this browser's local storage so a refresh does not lose your work. It lives on your device only, it is not synced, and clearing your browser data removes it. No one else can read it.

Exports

The PNG and ZIP files are generated inside your browser and saved straight to your computer. They do not pass through a server.

Third parties

None. Fonts are served from this site rather than a font CDN, so loading a page does not reveal your IP address to anyone else. There is no analytics, no advertising, no embedded media and no tracking of any kind.

Server logs

Like almost every website, whoever hosts this may keep standard access logs — typically IP address, browser user-agent, page requested and timestamp. These are produced by the hosting infrastructure for security and reliability, not created by us for marketing, and they are not connected to anything you make here. Retention depends on the host.

Your rights

There is no personal data of yours for us to hold, so there is nothing to request, correct or delete. If you contact us by email, we keep that correspondence in order to reply. To have it deleted, just ask.

If this changes

If the tool ever adds accounts, analytics or anything that collects data, this page will be updated before that ships — not after.
